# Getting the first 20–50 users

**Goal:** 20 real people build a Career DNA in the next 2 weeks, and 50 in 4 weeks. At least 10 of them tell you what they think.

"Real" means someone looking for a job or thinking about a change, not a friend clicking around to be nice. Friends are fine as a start if they send it on to someone who needs it.

## Who to reach first

Trazerr helps most when a resume undersells someone: people without a tech background, people changing fields, and people coming back to work. Start there:

1. **People you know who are job hunting** (and people they know).
2. **Job centers.** Every area has a free American Job Center. Find the nearest at careeronestop.org ("Find an American Job Center"). In New Jersey they're listed at careerconnections.nj.gov. Staff there help many job seekers every week and are always looking for free tools.
3. **Community college and university career offices**, and adult education programs.
4. **Public libraries.** Many run job-search help sessions.
5. **Online groups:** local Facebook job groups, LinkedIn, and Reddit (r/resumes, r/jobs, r/careerguidance, r/findapath). Read each group's rules first; many only allow tools if you're open about being the maker.

## The two-week plan

| When | Do this | Aim |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Send the personal message below to 15 people you know. Ask each to forward it to one person who is job hunting. | 5 users |
| Day 2 | Post on LinkedIn (draft below). | 5 users |
| Days 3–5 | Email 5 job centers or career offices (draft below). Follow up by phone after 3 days. | 1 partner |
| Days 5–7 | One Reddit post, in a group that allows it (draft below). Reply to every comment. | 5 users |
| Week 2 | Offer a 15-minute call to everyone who used it. Watch them use it if they'll share a screen. | 5 calls |
| Every Friday | Open the usage page. Note visits, Career DNAs built, errors and feedback. Fix the top problem. | |

## How you'll know it's working

Check `https://trazerr.com/stats.html` weekly:

- **Visits that started a Career DNA:** if under 10%, the first screen isn't convincing people to try.
- **Career DNAs that succeeded:** should be above 90%. If not, check Recent errors.
- **Feedback that was positive:** read every comment, good or bad.

Keep a simple list (a spreadsheet is fine): who, where they came from, what they said, what you changed.

## Messages you can send

Edit these to sound like you. Don't promise things Trazerr doesn't do.

### A message to people you know

> Hi [name], I built a free website called Trazerr (trazerr.com). You upload your resume and it shows the strengths it sees in you, including ones your resume only hints at, then finds jobs that fit and checks how well you fit each one.
>
> I'm looking for a few people to try it and tell me honestly what's confusing or wrong. Would you try it, or send it to someone you know who's job hunting? It takes about 2 minutes. Thank you!

### LinkedIn post

> I've been building Trazerr, a free tool for job seekers: trazerr.com
>
> Most resumes undersell people. Trazerr reads yours and shows your Career DNA: what your resume shows, what it suggests between the lines, and roles worth exploring. Then it searches real openings near you, checks your fit for each one, and helps you tailor your resume without making anything up.
>
> It's free and you don't need an account. I'm looking for the first 50 people to try it and tell me what works and what doesn't. If you're job hunting, or know someone who is, I'd be grateful if you tried it or passed it on.

### Reddit post (only where the rules allow it)

> Title: I made a free tool that shows what your resume says about you. Looking for honest feedback.
>
> I'm the maker, so I'm biased. Trazerr (trazerr.com) reads your resume and lists your strengths in plain language: what it shows, what it suggests, and roles worth exploring. It also searches real jobs near you and checks your fit for each one. Free, no sign-up.
>
> I'd really like to hear what's confusing, wrong or missing. Please remove your name and contact details from your resume before trying it if you prefer.

### Follow-up to someone who tried it

> Thanks for trying Trazerr! Three quick questions, one line each is plenty:
>
> 1. Did the Career DNA describe you accurately? What was off?
> 2. Was anything confusing?
> 3. Would you use it again, or tell a friend about it? Why or why not?
