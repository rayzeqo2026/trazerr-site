Privacy policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

The short version
  • Trazerr doesn't keep your resume. It's sent to Claude, Anthropic's AI, to produce your results, and that's it.
  • If you save your Career DNA, it stays in your browser on your device, along with your resume so you can tailor it later.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your information.
  • The only personal information we store is your email address, and only if you join the waitlist.

Who we are

Trazerr (trazerr.com) helps people understand what their work history proves and where it can take them. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the site. Questions go to hello@trazerr.com.

Your resume and other text you submit

When you build your Career DNA, check your fit for a job, plan a career path, use Tailor my DNA or build a Job DNA, the text or file you provide is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's API so Claude can produce the result. The result comes back to your browser.

Trazerr does not save a copy of your resume, the job postings you paste, your tailored resume, or the results. Anthropic processes the content to return the result, under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

Word (.docx) files are read in your own browser, and only the extracted text is sent. PDFs are sent as the file itself.

Please don't include information you don't want processed, such as government ID numbers or health details. A resume doesn't need them.

What stays on your device

If you choose to save your Career DNA, it's stored in your browser's local storage on that device, along with your resume so you can use Tailor my DNA later without uploading it again. Neither is sent to us unless you ask for something that needs it, like a fit check, a career path or a tailored resume, and then only for that request. You can delete both at any time with "Remove from this device" in your results, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Career DNA cards and tailored resume files are made on your device. They're never uploaded to Trazerr.

Job search

When you search for jobs, your search words and location are sent to our job listing partners, Adzuna and Remotive, to find openings. Search results may be cached briefly so repeat searches load faster. When you open a job, you go to that site, and its privacy policy applies there.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and the date you signed up. We use it only to tell you about early access and new features, and we don't share or sell it. To be removed, email hello@trazerr.com.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request details when you visit, and keeps standard server logs. To stop any one visitor from sending too many requests, we keep a scrambled, one-way code made from your IP address, together with a request count, for 10 minutes. The code can't be turned back into your IP address. Our error logs record what went wrong with a request, not the contents of your resume.

To improve Trazerr, we count anonymous events, like how many Career DNAs are built each day. Those counts are just an event name and a daily total. They include no cookies, names, IP addresses or resume content.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and loads a Word file reader from cdnjs only when you upload a .docx file. Those services see your IP address when your browser requests the files.

If you answer "Was this accurate?" on a result, we keep your thumbs up or down, which screen it was about, the role (if any) and any comment you type, so we can improve Trazerr. We don't keep your resume with it, so please leave personal details out of comments.

We don't use advertising, analytics trackers or tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

You can use Trazerr without giving us any personal information. Because we don't store resumes or results, there's nothing for us to delete after you close the page. For anything related to your waitlist email, including a copy or deletion, contact hello@trazerr.com and we'll respond promptly.

Children

Trazerr is meant for people aged 16 and over who are looking for work or planning a career.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, such as when accounts launch, we'll say so clearly on the site.
