    How we handle your data.

    When you build your Career DNA, check a fit or plan a path, the text is sent over an encrypted connection to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which returns the result. Trazerr does not keep a copy of your resume on its servers.

    If you choose to save your Career DNA, it's stored in this browser only. It never leaves your device except when you ask for a fit check or a path, and you can remove it at any time from your results.

    Job listings come from third-party job boards. When you open a job, you go to that board's site, and their privacy policy applies there.

    If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address only to tell you about early access. We don't sell it or share it.

    Career DNA cards are made on your device and never uploaded. To improve Trazerr, we count anonymous events, like how many Career DNAs are built each day. Those counts include no cookies, names, IP addresses or resume content.
