    Why Trazerr exists.

    Resumes undersell people, and hiring tools reward keywords over evidence. Trazerr reads a career the way a good mentor would: carefully, honestly, and on your side.

    Evidence first

    Every strength points to a line in your resume. Anything we infer is labeled, with the reasoning shown.

    Honest about what we don't know

    Gaps and unknowns are shown, not hidden. We never add a skill, title or number you didn't give us.

    Yours to keep

    We don't store your resume. Your Career DNA stays on your device unless you ask for something that needs it.

    How we handle your data.

    When you build your Career DNA, check a fit or plan a path, the text is sent over an encrypted connection to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which returns the result. Trazerr does not keep a copy of your resume on its servers.

    If you choose to save your Career DNA, it's stored in this browser only, along with your resume so you can tailor it later. Neither leaves your device except when you ask for a fit check, a path or a tailored resume, and you can remove both at any time from your results.

    Job listings come from third-party job boards. When you open a job, you go to that board's site, and their privacy policy applies there.

    If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address only to tell you about early access. We don't sell it or share it.

    Career DNA cards are made on your device and never uploaded. To improve Trazerr, we count anonymous events, like how many Career DNAs are built each day. Those counts include no cookies, names, IP addresses or resume content.
